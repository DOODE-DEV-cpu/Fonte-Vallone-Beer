La Nostra Storia

Where the mountain
meets the glass.

High on the slopes of Monte Morrone, the medieval village of Pacentro has watched over the Peligna valley for nearly a thousand years. Its stone lanes, its three Caldora towers and the cold, clear water that runs down from the Maiella are the soul of every bottle we make.

Fonte Vallone is our tribute to this land: a small-batch brewery that refuses shortcuts. We brew slowly, in limited quantities, with mountain spring water and carefully selected malts and hops, so that every glass tastes of the place it comes from.

Il Metodo

Four elements. No shortcuts.

  1. 01

    L'Acqua

    Cold, mineral-light spring water from the Maiella massif — the foundation of a clean, crisp body.

  2. 02

    Il Malto

    Carefully selected barley malts, gently mashed to reveal notes of bread crust, honey and toasted grain.

  3. 03

    Il Luppolo

    Noble aroma hops added by hand at precise moments, for balance, perfume and a refined bitterness.

  4. 04

    Il Tempo

    Long, cool maturation and natural refermentation in the bottle. Patience is our final ingredient.
